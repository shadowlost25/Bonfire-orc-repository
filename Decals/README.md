# How to download and apply decals.
Applying decals work similarly to tattoos and scars. At the moment, you will need to speak to Kult, and get him to apply the decal as a Tattoo, but you can then toggle it off after that.

To apply the decal to a piece of equipment:
1. First equip the clothing item.
2. Click "Edit worn item colors" on the bottom right corner of the inventory screen and click the clothing item.
3. Decals show up on all three color layers (Cloth, Plate, Leather), so ignore that, anc click the blue button next to it, labeled "Edit custom decals". This will display a mini winddow showing the worn item's UV map.
4. The dropdown list on this page will contain all of the tattoo images currently on your character. Click the "tattoo" you intend to use as a decal.
5. Drag the tattoo around the UVs, and this will update your clothing piece in real time. You can adjust the and rotation of the item with the below fields:
    - **Top**: Rotation in degrees. Accepts negative values.
    - **Middle**: Horizontal Tiling. This sort of acts as the X scale, but larger values will shrink the image.
    - **Bottom**: Vertical Tiling. This likewise acts as the Y scale.
    - If you mess up any of these values and lose your decal in the workspace, you can click "Reset Decal Positioning" to reset the values to the default, and re-center your decal on the window.
6. Click "Done" and the decal will remain on your character's clothing item, even if you unequip it! Don't forget to save afterwards.
