**Note**: Applying a tattoo and a scar together can be used to give the tattoo image some depth

# How to download and apply tattoos/scars
1. In the Outback, speak to Kult at Kult's Grave
2. On his dialogue menu, click "Services" and then "Tattoos"
3. The next screen will attempt to pull up a list of tattoos stored in your Bonfire Appdata directory. 
    - If none are found, click "Files", and this will open a file browser window to your Tattoos folder. 
    - Drag and drop any tattoo files you've downloaded from here or elsewhere.
    - Click "Refresh", and Bonfire will attempt to populate the Tattoos list
4. Click the desired tattoo
5. Click "Apply" to apply the tattoo to your current orc, or click "Scar" to instead apply the tattoo as a scar.
6. The tattoo or scar will also appear in your inventory. Right-clicking itw ill toggle it on or off. Clicking and dragging it out of your inventory will delete it.
